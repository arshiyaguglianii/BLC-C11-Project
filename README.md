# BLC_C11 

## About the Project

BLC_C11 is a Data Analysis project focused on analysing business listings, customer reviews, ratings, categories and geographical distribution. The project uses data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, machine learning and dashboard visualisation to extract meaningful business insights and identify potential leads.

## Objectives

- Clean and preprocess the raw business dataset.
- Standardise business categories.
- Classify businesses as Indian or Foreign.
- Analyse customer ratings and review patterns.
- Classify businesses into High, Medium and Low review categories.
- Develop a category-based lead prioritisation model.
- Create dashboards to visualise business trends and performance.
- Generate actionable business insights and recommendations.

## Tools & Technologies

- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- Scikit-learn
- Matplotlib
- Jupyter Notebook
- Microsoft Excel
- Power BI
- Tableau

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Cleaning & Preprocessing
The raw business dataset was cleaned and processed by handling data quality issues, standardising business categories and preparing the data for further analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Data Analysis
Analysed business distribution across cities, countries and categories, along with customer ratings and review volumes to identify patterns and trends.

### 3. AI Review Analysis Model
Implemented a Random Forest Classifier to classify businesses into High, Medium and Low review categories based on their ratings and review counts.

### 4. AI Category Analysis & Lead Prioritisation
Developed a category-based machine learning model using a Random Forest Regressor to assign businesses different lead priority levels based on category-related scores and features.

### 5. Dashboard Development
Created dashboards using Excel, Power BI and Tableau to visualise:
- Business distribution across cities and countries.
- Category-wise business distribution.
- Average ratings by category.
- Customer review patterns.
- Overall business performance and trends.

## Key Analysis Areas

- Business distribution and geographical trends.
- Category-wise ratings and reviews.
- Customer feedback analysis.
- High, Medium and Low review classification.
- Category-based lead prioritisation.
- Business performance comparisons.

## Project Outcomes

The project combines data analysis, machine learning and data visualisation to provide insights into business listings and customer feedback. The final outputs include cleaned datasets, review classification results, lead priority predictions and interactive dashboards to support business analysis and lead identification.
